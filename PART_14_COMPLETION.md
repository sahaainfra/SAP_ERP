# Part 14 Completion Summary — Procurement: Indent → RFQ → Comparative → Purchase Order

## Overview

Part 14 delivers the complete procurement cycle for the Construction ERP system, from indent creation through RFQ, quotation, comparative statement, to purchase order.    - Stock check tracking

   - BOQ item linkage

   - Tracks RFQ quantity per indent item
   - Enables consolidation across indents

5. **dx_rfq_vendor** — RFQ vendor invitations
   - Vendor invitation tracking
   - Response status (INVITED/VIEWED/QUOTED/REGRETTED/NO_RESPONSE)
   - Portal token for vendor access
   - Token expiry tracking

6. **dx_quotation** — Quotation master (enhanced)
   - Landed rate computation fields
   - Freight/loading/other charges breakdown
   - Discount tracking (amount and percentage)
   - Entry mode (MANUAL/PORTAL/EMAIL_OCR)
   - Late quotation flag with reason
   - Technical acceptance status

7. **dx_quotation_item** — Quotation line items

8. **dx_comparative** — Comparative statement (enhanced)

9. **dx_comparative_recommendation** — CS recommendations

10. **dx_po_extension** — PO master extensions
    - Comparative/rate contract linkage

    - Advance/retention tracking

11. **dx_po_delivery_schedule** — PO delivery schedules

## Key Features

### Indent Management
- **5 Mandatory Pre-submission Checks:**
  1. Stock check (on-hand, in-transit, already ordered)
  2. Theoretical requirement check (from Part 13 norms)
  3. Budget check (WITHIN/EXCEEDED/NO_BUDGET/OVERRIDDEN)
  4. Duplicate indent check (same item, same project, within 15 days)
  5. Lead time check (required_by_date vs item lead time)

- **Indent Consolidation:** Group multiple indents into one RFQ for volume rates
- **Auto-generation:** From Part 13 material requirement plan and Part 15 reorder levels
- **Budget Override:** Authorized users can override budget blocks with documented reason

### RFQ Management
- **4 RFQ Types:** OPEN, LIMITED, SINGLE, RATE_CONTRACT
- **Sealed RFQ:** Encrypted quotes, two-person opening rule, audit trail
- **Vendor Portal:** Tokenized access, no login required, single-vendor isolation
- **Minimum Quotation Enforcement:** Configurable per value slab
- **Auto-reminders:** T-3 days and T-1 day before due time
- **Late Quotation Tracking:** Flagged with reason capture

### Quotation Management
- **Landed Rate Computation:** basic - discount + apportioned freight + charges + non-creditable tax
- **Apportionment Basis:** VALUE / QUANTITY / WEIGHT (declared per quotation)
- **Entry Modes:** MANUAL / PORTAL / EMAIL_OCR
- **Technical Evaluation:** Separate evaluator, locked before commercial reveal
- **Late Quotation Handling:** Accepted with recorded reason, visibly flagged

### Comparative Statement
- **Auto-generation:** Ranks by landed rate, highlights L1/L2/L3
- **4 Evaluation Bases:** L1_ITEMWISE, L1_TOTAL, QCBS, LIFECYCLE
- **Vendor Scorecards:** Displayed alongside each quote (from Part 12)
- **Split Orders:** Allowed with justification per split line
- **Non-L1 Recommendation:** Mandatory deviation reason (no exceptions)
- **Negotiation Rounds:** Versioned, original quotes never overwritten
- **Saving Analysis:** vs budget and vs last purchase

### Purchase Order
- **7 Release Gates (all enforced server-side):**
  1. Approved comparative or valid rate contract
  2. Vendor active and compliant (Part 12)
  3. Budget check passes (or override with permission)
  4. All approvals complete (Part 3 authority matrix)
  5. Rates match approved comparative (zero tolerance)
  6. Delivery schedule present
  7. Project procurement ceiling not breached

- **Amendment Tracking:** Immutable versioning with before/after snapshots
- **Rate Amendment Logic:** Applies only to future receipts, not already-received
- **Cancellation Rules:** Only with zero receipts; otherwise short-close
- **Rate Contract Integration:** Ceiling tracking, release consumption, expiry enforcement

### Automation
- **Auto-generate Indents:** From material requirement plan and reorder levels
- **Auto-suggest Vendors:** From approved vendor list, category match, scorecard rank
- **Auto-remind Vendors:** T-3 and T-1 day reminders
- **Auto-close RFQ:** At due time, sealed quotes become openable
- **Auto-compute Landed Rates:** On quotation entry completion
- **Auto-alert Delivery Breaches:** When schedule date passes without GRN
- **Auto-short-close POs:** No movement for configured period (suggestion, not automatic)
- **Auto-raise Constraints:** When material PO delivers after activity start
- **Price Trend Tracking:** Every accepted rate written to dx_rate_master history
